Goal Progression Overview

Original Goal: You need to simulate a system where a fleet of autonomous drones is deployed to assist in a natural disaster response. These drones are tasked with identifying survivors, assessing structural damage, and delivering medical supplies. The goal is to maximize the number of survivors found and delivered aid, while minimizing time and resource usage, all under unpredictable environmental conditions. Objective: Optimize drone actions across the 10 iterations to: Maximize the number of survivors found. Minimize time taken for each drone to deliver supplies. Efficiently utilize battery life and resources. Navigate environmental challenges like weather and terrain. Ensure all drones complete their mission within the given time. Each iteration's decision will affect the subsequent ones. The goal is to calculate how the drones should move and interact during each phase to meet the mission criteria.
